The Studio

Bucle is a residential design practice based in San Salvador. We take on a small number of projects at a time so each one gets the attention it needs.

Designed for how you live

We start with routines, not references. How you cook, host, work and rest sets the plan before a single finish is chosen.

Made close to home

Wood, textiles and joinery come from Salvadoran workshops wherever we can. It keeps quality in view and the craft in the country.

One team, start to finish

Design, drawings and municipal permits are handled in-house, so nothing falls between an architect and a paperwork office.

How we work

  1. 01

    Conversation

    We talk through the space, the budget and the timeline. No cost, no commitment.

  2. 02

    Design

    Layouts, materials and 3D views, revised with you until the plan is right.

  3. 03

    Documentation

    Construction drawings and, where needed, the municipal permit submission.

  4. 04

    Build

    We stay involved on site so what gets built matches what was drawn.
